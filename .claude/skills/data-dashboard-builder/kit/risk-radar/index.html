How the risk score works
  • Only accounts with at least 5 cases in the last 90 days are scored.
  • The score runs from 0 to 100. SLA breach rate gives up to 35 points (full at 40% of cases breached). Low CSAT gives up to 30 points, on a 1 to 5 scale. When fewer than 3 customers rated their cases, CSAT counts as neutral. The share of Critical and High priority cases gives up to 20 points (full at 50%). Cases linked to a platform incident give up to 15 points (full at 3 cases).
  • Bands: High is 60 or more, Elevated is 40 to 59, Watch is below 40.
  • Top driver is the part of the score that adds the most points.
  • All figures come from the Salesforce data in Databricks (workspace.salesforce_synth), with the same definitions as the risk-radar skill.
